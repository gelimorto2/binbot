the safety circuit, control and communications, the electronics box (E‑box),
fuses, wires and connectors. The sensors themselves (LiDAR, GNSS, cameras)
are detailed in BP‑05; here they are wired in.

because the front drives under bins on the base stands (BP‑02).

GNSS1 and GNSS2 are 1 m apart across the robot, which gives the heading.

## 3. How it works

- **Power (E‑01):** battery → 200 A fuse → key isolator → **main contactor K1** →
  +48 V DRIVE bus → motor controllers and lift. Aux supplies (computer, sensors,
  radio) are taken **before** K1, so the robot can still report its position and
  status after an E‑stop.
- **Safety (E‑02):** 4 E‑stops, wireless E‑stop, both safety edges and the software
  watchdog are wired in **two channels** into a safety relay. Any one of them opens
  K1 (no drive, no lift) and K3 (brake coils lose power → spring brakes clamp).
  Restart needs the blue RESET button. A welded contactor blocks the reset.
- **Control (E‑03):** the computer plans and drives; the MCU does real‑time
  I/O (motors over CAN, lift, weighing, display, LoRa pendant, watchdog).
  If the computer hangs, the MCU stops the robot. If the MCU hangs, its watchdog
  relay K4 opens the safety chain.

## 4. Power budget

| Rail | Loads | Typical | Peak | Supply |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 48 V DRIVE | 2 motors | 1.5 kW | 8 kW (170 A, seconds) | F1 200 A time‑delay |
| 24 V LIFT | 2–4 actuators | 0 | 480 W, ~20 s per swap | U3 600 W |
| 24 V CTRL | PC1 15, LiDAR 7, cameras 6, GNSS 2, MCU+relays 10, display 5, switch 4, brakes 40 (driving only) | 50 W parked / 90 W driving | 120 W | U1 240 W |
| 12 V COMMS | router 6, LoRa 1, beacon 8, buzzer 2 | 10 W | 17 W | U2 60 W |

Parked at a spot for 8 h ≈ 0.5 kWh, small next to a ~7.7 kWh battery.
**All DC/DCs must accept 36–75 V input** (LiFePO4 16S runs 40–58.4 V).

## 8. Tests before first drive

| # | Test | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Insulation: battery + and − to frame, isolator open | > 1 MΩ |
| E2 | CAN bus resistance, power off | 60 Ω ± 5 |
| E3 | Each E‑stop, the wireless E‑stop, each safety edge, watchdog: press / trigger **one at a time** while the motors run on stands | K1 and K3 drop, brakes clamp, reset needed |
| E4 | Wireless E‑stop out of range / battery removed | Robot stops within 0.5 s |
| E5 | Bridge K1 aux (simulated weld), try reset | Reset refused |
| E6 | Unplug PC1 USB while driving on stands | MCU stops the motors |
| E7 | Charger plugged in | Drive refused |
| E8 | 2 h run in the sun, E‑box closed | Inside < 50 °C |

**Have the safety circuit (E‑02) and the risk assessment checked by a qualified
person (ISO 13849) before anyone works next to the robot.**
